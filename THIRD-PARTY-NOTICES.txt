AnmolLipi / GurmukhiAkhar ↔ Unicode Gurmukhi conversion engine

The legacy ASCII mapping, conversion sequence and Unicode normalization logic in assets/js/anmollipi-converter.js are adapted from the MIT-licensed Gurmukhi package by Shabad OS:
https://github.com/shabados/shabados/tree/main/packages/gurmukhi
Copyright (c) 2026 Shabad OS contributors.

The upstream package documents this legacy ASCII encoding as used by older Gurmukhi fonts including AnmolLipi and GurmukhiAkhar. Its conversion is lossy for some variant distinctions; the UnicodeFix implementation targets Unicode Consortium Gurmukhi output. The MIT license terms and attribution are retained in the converter source.
